# Short screenplay: The Washing Machine's Confession
**Genre**: Realism / Silent comedy / Psychological realism / Mild absurdism  
**Duration**: Approximately 6 minutes  
**Plot points**: 6  
**Directorial sensibility**: Hirokazu Kore-eda × Santayana's everyday philosophy

---

## Overall tone
- **Central idea**: Through repetitive routines, people project absurd fantasies onto lifeless objects, finally seeing their loneliness and tenderness in silence.
- **Visual style**: Minimal domestic aesthetics, desaturated realism, asymmetrical arrangements of people and objects.
- **Narrative approach**: Linear reality interspersed with fantasy as a parallel narrative. Their mismatch produces humor and sadness.
- **Point of view**: First-person interior perspective, with third-person observation of objects' behavior.
- **Music**: Minimal single-line piano, like Ryuichi Sakamoto, slow with slightly displaced electronic beats that suggest fractured thinking.
- **Emotional arc**: Calm → absurd self-mockery → quiet acceptance → inner tenderness → sadness without injury → quiet reconciliation.
- **Devices**: Fantasy uses fixed shots and slow movement, contrasting with reality to simulate circling thoughts. The washing machine's human-like voice triggers every fantasy. Drops, rotating dial, automatic shutdown, and beeps recur. Its “13th restart” is foreshadowed and revealed only at the end.

## Characters
| Name | Identity | Appearance | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lin Xiaoyu | 27, ordinary office worker | Small and thin, faded cotton shirt, casually wavy hair, rimless glasses, thin silver ring on left ring finger | Introverted, habitually talks to herself, small daily rituals; neutral expression, sensitive eyes |
| Washing machine | No identity; the household's central appliance | Black metal, matte gray texture, two small lights, retro-red dial; numbered strip around it says “13th restart” | Unconsciously “thinks,” “talks,” “plans work,” and “complains about life”; a projection of her imagination |

## Screenplay

### S01: Home late · INT./EXT. · Night ｜ [00:00–00:18]

*“Every day when I get home, I check whether the washing machine is working. Not because it's washing clothes. I'm afraid it will forget who it is.”*

Lin Xiaoyu drags a suitcase inside, key tapping the lock. The machine is still, its dial suspended “in midair,” reflecting faint window light. She runs her fingertips along its metal texture as if reading an expired diary.

    LIN XIAOYU (softly, slowly): No washing again. Guess you're still asleep today.

*“It never cries or makes a fuss. It just waits there, like a phone that's waited ten years for me.”*

Sound: lock click, air-conditioning breeze, distant bark, one faint drop.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Medium shot, static hold, slight push.
> - Composition: Eye level turning overhead; machine lower left, person standing front right.
> - Lighting: Warm-yellow ceiling light and cold-blue window glow, low contrast, repression.
> - Color: 3200K, 20% saturation, gray-blue and cream; calm with distance.
> - Atmosphere: Loneliness → composure → hidden anticipation.
> - Music: Intermittent single piano notes, like a variation on the *Weathering with You* opening.
> - Transition: Enter on the lock; exit on an extended water drop.
> - Narrative function: Establish their initial relationship and the psychological contract between person and object.

### S02: Pouring water · Kitchen · Day ｜ [00:19–00:35]

*“I made instant noodles today. It said, ‘That smells like your dad when he was little.’”*

She pours water into a white porcelain cup, clear drops ringing. Watching the splash, she seems to await an answer. The tap drips steadily. Suddenly she stops and studies the old clock on the left wall.

    LIN XIAOYU (to herself, smiling): You say you pour water every morning. You've never asked what I'm eating today.

She twists the tap. Water continues. Suddenly the machine's door creaks.

Sound: repeated drips, a lightly turned tap, faint click of the machine opening.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld follow, fixed medium shot, slight raised gesture as the camera withdraws.
> - Composition: Low camera and overhead water view; still machine at rear right forms a diagonal.
> - Lighting: Soft natural sidelight, cold-blue water reflections; freshness slightly displaced.
> - Color: 4000K, 30% saturation, white and pale blue; lightness conceals estrangement.
> - Atmosphere: Everyday cheer gives way to silent absurdity.
> - Music: Piano strengthens; low electronic edge disappears after 0.5 seconds.
> - Transition: Enter on drips; exit through the machine's door click.
> - Narrative function: Foreshadow the machine's response and the possibility of hearing everyday objects.

### S03: Imagined conversation · Living room · Night ｜ [00:36–00:57]

*“If it could talk, it wouldn't say ‘Wash complete.’ It would say, ‘You didn't wash your hair today. I handled it for you.’”*

On the sofa, Lin Xiaoyu glances at her lit phone and turns it off. The camera slowly enters the machine. Its door opens gently. A low, tired human voice:

    WASHING MACHINE (calmly, slowly): You forgot shampoo again. I counted. Seven times you haven't washed your hair. I remember. This is the seventh.

She stiffens and turns toward it. Its dial slowly turns with a faint hum.

*“It wasn't talking about clothes. It was talking about my life.”*

Sound: machine hum, one live-action water plunk, indistinct distant subway.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Static shot, move across the dial, shaking close shot.
> - Composition: Symmetrical medium shot, centered machine, person beside it; coexistence.
> - Lighting: Warm-yellow sidelight, cold-gray dial backlight, bordering light and dark; absurd yet real.
> - Color: 2800K, 25% saturation, beige-yellow and pale gray; calm slides into absurdity.
> - Atmosphere: Absurdity → inner uncertainty → emotional resonance.
> - Music: Piano stops for 1 second, then restarts on single notes.
> - Transition: Water drop in; fading speaker hum out.
> - Narrative function: Open the first fantasy; establish the machine as an emotional vessel.

### S04: The dial's schedule · Bedroom · Night ｜ [00:58–1:15]

*“It says it will schedule my work, as if I too am someone who needs managing.”*

Beside the bed, she opens old albums. In a college group photo she beams beside a roommate. At the corner: “Don't forget the laundry.” She freezes.

The machine speaks again, this time from the refrigerator.

    WASHING MACHINE (calm, decisive): Tomorrow at nine, you must be at work. The boss says the project team is bloated. I recommend resigning, because you're the only one who can soothe the team's emotions.

Stunned, she turns pages and discovers the same small line in every photo's corner: “Did the laundry today.”

*“At last I understood. It wasn't reminding me to wash clothes. It was reminding me to live.”*

Sound: turning pages, album paper cracking, repeating machine beep.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slow pan, close-up of album text, tracking her movements.
> - Composition: Low angle, album right, machine left, opposing thoughts.
> - Lighting: Warm-yellow curtain light and cold-white desk lamp split the image; absurdity becomes introspection.
> - Color: 3000K, 28% saturation, yellow and white; satire softens.
> - Atmosphere: Satire → reflection → heartache.
> - Music: Piano suddenly stops for 3 seconds, then loops single notes.
> - Transition: Page turn in; repeated machine beep out.
> - Narrative function: Reveal the machine's control of life's emotions and turn the psychological arc.

### S05: The boss's resignation · Counter · Day ｜ [1:16–1:42]

*“Today at the office I saw a resignation letter folded into a washing machine.”*

She enters the office in glaring sunlight and retrieves an unsent letter from a drawer. Crumpled paper, one corner compressed into an abstract cube. She unfolds it:

“I've decided to leave. The team is too noisy, like an endless wash cycle.”

Stunned, she approaches the window. Sunlight sweeps across the page; suddenly she laughs.

    LIN XIAOYU (softly, smiling): So you planned to quit even before I did.

She refolds it and returns it to the drawer. As she turns, the washing-machine door opens gently with a beep.

Sound: paper friction, typing, quiet air-conditioning, clear machine beep.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Push into letter, pull out to reality, linger on an empty shot.
> - Composition: Eye-level letter, desks behind; reality and fantasy juxtaposed.
> - Lighting: Strong cold-white sidelight and deep-gray paper shadows; forceful contrast.
> - Color: 5000K, 40% saturation, white and gray; oppression becomes relief.
> - Atmosphere: Eruption → release → inner reconciliation.
> - Music: Light electronic rhythm enters, returning to the original piano key after 3 seconds.
> - Transition: Paper friction in; live-action machine beep out.
> - Narrative function: Fantasy and reality intersect, answering foreshadowing and completing an emotional loop.

---

### S06: Shutdown · Living room · Night ｜ [1:43–2:00]

*“At last it stopped talking. Not because it broke. It finally understood that some things need no explanation.”*

Lin Xiaoyu watches the still dial from the sofa. She takes an old key and inserts it into the machine's power port. Power snaps off.

She smiles and whispers:

    LIN XIAOYU (tenderly): I heard you say so much. Really, I knew all along you were waiting for me.

Time stops. Drops slowly gather into a little puddle. The camera rises; outside is faintly bright.

Sound: one final drip. No other sound.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: God's-eye view, overhead drops, slow push toward the window.
> - Composition: High angle, machine centered, person bent low in submission.
> - Lighting: Brightening cold-blue sky, mirrored water reflections, softened light and tender emotion.
> - Color: 4500K, 15% saturation, blue and white; peace and lingering feeling.
> - Atmosphere: Quiet → tenderness → long aftertaste.
> - Music: Soft repeated single piano note fades to silence over 3 seconds.
> - Transition: Enter on a drop; slowly withdraw and disappear into the window.
> - Narrative function: Emotional closure, elevating the relationship between person and object, resonance continuing.
