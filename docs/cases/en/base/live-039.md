# Short screenplay: Broken Glass at the Street Corner
**Genre**: Realism / Handheld photography / Marginalized youth / Emotional settling  
**Duration**: Approximately 6 minutes  
**Plot points**: 6  
**Directorial sensibility**: The Dardenne brothers × cyberpunk marginal realism

---

## Overall tone
- **Central idea**: A tiny mistake expands without limit in a poor neighborhood, consuming everyone's perception of reality and leaving an empty space of glass and silence.
- **Visual style**: Slightly shaking handheld shots near the ground or from oblique low angles; blurred edges, with dust, raindrops, and wrinkled clothes intruding.
- **Narrative approach**: Linear, event-driven, showing behavior and consequences without explaining motivation; event → response → emptiness.
- **Point of view**: First person, a local delinquent with a narrow perspective and volatile emotions, gradually losing touch with reality.
- **Music**: Slow low electronic pulses, intermittent phone rings, traffic and distant radio static; no melody, like the city breathing.
- **Emotional arc**: Anger → forced response → fracture → self-blame → indifference → emptiness.
- **Devices**: Repeated scenes, such as pouring from a glass or a wall crack, stress cycles. Dialogue is brief and broken, as if recorded through earphones. Glass shards develop from physical objects into symbols.

## Characters
| Name | Identity | Appearance | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiao Kai | 17, neighborhood delinquent, accidental participant | 17, tall and thin, ripped jeans, scorched left sleeve, deformed headphones | Irritable, sensitive, uses violence for small problems; slight tendency toward self-harm; secretly checks old phone photos |
| Old Wang | 40, neighborhood repairman | 50, oil-stained work clothes, limping right leg, rusty iron bucket on his back | Taciturn as a wall, always watches warily |
| Unnamed girl | 8, lives at the alley's end | Unraveling green dress, messy hair, always holding a moldy cardboard box | Unconsciously observes, sometimes suddenly looks into the camera with a fixed gaze |

## Scene list
| Scene | Location | Time | Setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01: Corner pool | Abandoned city corner, concrete pool | Dusk | Low angle, puddles, peeling walls, gray-blue sky with sunset warmth; dust in light shafts; half a plastic bottle and glass float in the pool |

## Screenplay

### S01: Corner pool · INT./EXT. · Dusk ｜ [00:00–00:15]

*“I always feel somebody left me behind. Like that day, when I saw him—Old Wang—by the water, fixing a broken vase. I should have walked away. Instead I watched for three minutes.”*

{From ground level, the camera slowly approaches the pool. Glass fragments line its rim; a crack runs inward. Xiao Kai crouches, touches the cold water. Following his finger, the camera finds a small floating reflection.}

    XIAO KAI (breathless, eyes flickering): Nobody cares the vase broke… But looking at it is like looking at my mum.

*“He fixed it, then threw it away. Later I learned it belonged to his daughter.”*

Sound: wind, dripping, distant motorcycle engine shutting off, intermittent distant child crying.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld low angle, push in, aim toward the water.
> - Composition: Low angle with a recessed foreground, hand and shards forming the arrangement.
> - Lighting: Side-backlight, cold gray, low contrast; water reflections intensify emotion.
> - Color: Desaturated blue-gray, dominated by metallic silver and gray-green.
> - Atmosphere: Oppression → heaviness → forgotten senses.
> - Music: Slow low electronic pulse, one pause every 3 seconds.
> - Transition: Enter on a water reflection; the sound of breaking water tears the image apart.
> - Narrative function: Establish glass as the central symbol, foreshadowing brokenness and memory.

### S02: Shoe repair · INT./EXT. · Day ｜ [00:16–00:32]

*“These past few days I keep dreaming of her in a green dress by the wall. She says, ‘Don't touch that vase.’ I didn't listen.”*

{Close to Old Wang on his wooden stool, left hand holding a hammer, right holding a sole, moving slowly. Behind him Xiao Kai keeps checking his watch; his mouth twitches.}

    OLD WANG (low, without looking at him): Three days and these shoes need replacing. Live long enough and people are shoes too. Once cracked, they can't be worn again.

*“Suddenly I remembered the last time I saw her, eight years ago. She smiled. I thought she would say, ‘Xiao Kai, grow up to be a good person.’”*

Sound: hammer strikes, sliding nails, a distant billboard's murmuring broadcast.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed-point tracking, slight handheld sway, reverse push toward his hands.
> - Composition: Medium shot and hand close-ups, wall crack at upper right.
> - Lighting: Natural sidelight, strong contrast, elongated figure.
> - Color: Warm yellow, brown clothing edge, dark-brown sole; an unbalanced warm center.
> - Atmosphere: Stillness → oppression → hidden tension.
> - Music: Slow, melody-free broadcast static dominates.
> - Transition: Enter on heavy hammering; exit with a power-cut sound, without an image.
> - Narrative function: Develop the past and memory, suggesting a connection between the two.

### S03: Phone screen · INT./EXT. · Day ｜ [00:33–00:45]

*“Opening my phone is opening another world. But it only says, ‘Third police report.’ I'm not what matters. I'm just a name.”*

{Camera against Xiao Kai's pocket. The screen lights up: “Police report no. 3,” “Location: street-corner pool.” Flickering, shaking image.}

    XIAO KAI (muttering, fingers shaking): I shouldn't have told him… But I know he already knew I'd seen that vase.

*“The phone makes sound, but I can't hear it. It's recording. I don't even know what.”*

Sound: phone flicker, low buzz, screen turning off, a small cough.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Body-close macro, slight phone vibration, cut back to his pupils.
> - Composition: Overhead; screen fills two-thirds, his hand's back at the left edge.
> - Lighting: Cold screen glow contrasts with weak gray surroundings.
> - Color: Blue-white screen, gray-black environment; cold-blue, bloodless emotion.
> - Atmosphere: Unease → introspection → invaded consciousness.
> - Music: Faint phone-alarm echo for 3 seconds, then gone.
> - Transition: Enter on flicker; exit on an unseen phone dropping.
> - Narrative function: Ignite emotion: a small mistake is recorded by a system, setting off conflict.

### S04: Tipped glass · INT./EXT. · Night ｜ [00:46–01:08]

*“At the convenience store I saw Old Wang putting a glass on the ground. It tipped. I could have left. I stood watching water run from the rim like blood.”*

{Close to the entrance, the glass falls and liquid spreads under a distant streetlamp. Xiao Kai approaches abruptly, touches the cold rim, then retreats.}

    XIAO KAI (shaking voice): You said the vase was your daughter's… Why is this cup glass too?

*“I thought something had merely broken. Now I understood—every broken thing is somebody else's memory.”*

Sound: breaking glass, drops, ground echoes, his accelerating breath.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Low tracking, slow retreat, hold on drops remaining at the rim.
> - Composition: Overhead, drops forming a line, Old Wang's back behind.
> - Lighting: Warm-yellow streetlamp reflected in spilled water, colliding with cold ground light.
> - Color: Warm yellow and icy blue, dominated by cold blue; emotional tearing.
> - Atmosphere: Confusion → fright → abnormal symmetry.
> - Music: Melody-free electronic pulse suddenly accelerates like a heartbeat.
> - Transition: Enter on dripping; all sound stops, black for 0.5 seconds.
> - Narrative function: The event erupts; glass becomes a symbol and summons memory.

---

### S05: Wall crack returns · INT./EXT. · Night ｜ [01:09–01:30]

*“When I returned, the wall was cracked. Not a new crack. A wider one. Like the corner of his daughter's diary torn by wind.”*

{Slowly approach the wall. The crack widens, ash drifting from its edges. Old Wang looks up into the lens, motionless. Xiao Kai retreats, footsteps lighter.}

    OLD WANG (calm, not looking at the camera): You can see it clearly. But it broke long ago.

*“At last I understood. We weren't fighting. We were repeating the same mistake.”*

Sound: ash falling from the crack, faint howling wind, distant rain beginning.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Static long take, slight push, slow withdrawal to the whole crack.
> - Composition: Medium-long shot; crack fills one-third, Old Wang seated lower right.
> - Lighting: Natural diffusion and light from the crack create vague reflections.
> - Color: Gray-white with faint yellow, predominantly gray; emotion settling.
> - Atmosphere: Desolation → acceptance → silence unto death.
> - Music: Electronic pulse slows toward silence.
> - Transition: Enter on the widening crack; exit with drifting ash.
> - Narrative function: Reveal repetition and insolubility; emotional collapse.

---

### S06: Glass in the empty lot · INT./EXT. · Dawn ｜ [01:31–01:45]

*“The next day the corner was empty. The pool dry. Old Wang gone. The girl left her box in a corner. I stood looking at the glass as if looking at my childhood.”*

{The camera slowly surveys scattered glass, no wind. Xiao Kai crouches and touches a shard, fingertip trembling, then slowly withdraws. The girl looks up calmly.}

    XIAO KAI (softly): They're all broken. But did you see? They've been breaking all along.

*“At last I understood—it wasn't my fault. The world already knew we'd all break.”*

Sound: extremely faint wind, low tiny cracks of glass on the ground.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slow moving push, low-angle survey, leave the frame empty for 1 second.
> - Composition: Wide angle, unordered shards, blurred background.
> - Lighting: Weak low morning skylight, barely reflective glass.
> - Color: Extremely cold gray, zero saturation; pure white and gray, complete emptiness.
> - Atmosphere: Absolute stillness → dark void → an ownerless aftertaste.
> - Music: In the final 3 seconds, a pulse rises from zero and returns to zero.
> - Transition: Glass reflection in; black frame and black phone screen out, silent.
> - Narrative function: End in emptiness, without redemption, solution, or light.
