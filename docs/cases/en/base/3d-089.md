# Short screenplay: The Seventh Door in the Old House
**Genre:** Fantasy / prejudice / fantastic realism / folk fable  
**Duration:** Approximately 5 minutes  
**Plot points:** 6  
**Directorial sensibility:** Hirokazu Kore-eda × a gentle Sisyphean fable

## Overall tone

- **Central theme:** Prejudice winds around a door like vines; acceptance is the first floral scent on the wind when the seventh door opens gently.
- **Visual style:** Hand-drawn texture, old-photo montage, realistic macro shots; nostalgia with the feel of mottled ink.
- **Narrative technique:** Linear story with memory-fragment flashbacks, entering through the little spirit's viewpoint, adult prejudice serving as a reflecting mirror.
- **Perspective:** First-person recollections of the old caretaker interwoven with the spirit's perspective, two resonating threads.
- **Music:** Minimal piano, soft bronze bells, ambient wind; slow, breathing rhythm.
- **Emotional arc:** Fear → rejection → calm observation → tentative contact → understanding silence → tender resonance.
- **Special devices:** The seventh door recurs with each erosion of prejudice. Opening doors subtly shifts interior light, reconstructing understanding. The spirit never speaks; movement and spatial relationships express its value.

## Screenplay

### S01 Old house gate at night · INT. · Day / night ｜ [00:00–00:18]

*“I've guarded this door all my life. Guarded the rules. If anyone takes a wrong step, the door will sound, will shatter.”*

From far outside, the camera moves in. Wind passes the door crack with a faint creak. The caretaker presses his palm against the door, knuckles white. Snow grains drift beneath it, as though time were breathing. He looks down: his shadow is taller than him, slowly writhing into the doorway.

    CARETAKER (deep, hoarse): “The seventh door… No one has ever dared push it. Because it knows an outsider lives inside.”

*“‘Outsider’ cuts across my throat like a blade. For twenty years I've used it to frame everyone unlike me.”*

Sound: Wind through the gap, faint falling snow, one small bronze-bell tremor.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed → slight push into the door crack.
> - Composition: Low angle, door focal, figure compressed at right.
> - Lighting: Cold yellow oil lamp at left, thin halo, displaced silhouettes at the crack.
> - Color: Cool, desaturated gray-brown and iron-gray; oppressive.
> - Atmosphere: Fear, medium intensity.
> - Music: Single piano notes descending slowly, syncopated wind.
> - Transition: In—wind at the crack; out—faint blue-green flash inside.
> - Narrative function: Establish prejudice and the seventh door as taboo.

### S02 Caretaker's study · INT. · Night ｜ [00:19–00:35]

*“I'm not afraid of it. I'm afraid of seeing myself.”*

Books pile in the study. A yellow map shows seven doors, the sixth crossed out, the seventh circled as “The ninth kind of being.” The caretaker turns an old volume to: “The seventh door opens only at the touch of the nameless.”

    CARETAKER (softly, trembling): “The ninth kind… The little spirit they talk about. The substitute Master buried in the cellar all those years ago.”

He looks up. Light flashes in a corner like an eye, then vanishes. He immediately lowers his head, afraid of being seen.

Sound: Turning pages, paper friction, one indistinct distant drop.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Push to page → slow pullback to map → overhead stacked books.
> - Composition: Medium, book centered, figure peripheral, only map lit.
> - Lighting: Single dim yellow source, low contrast, gray halo.
> - Color: Grayed warm yellow, off-white and aged brown; oppression with memory.
> - Atmosphere: Reflection and self-questioning, medium-high intensity.
> - Music: Piano diminishes as wind enters.
> - Transition: In—map glimmers; out—blue-green light in door crack.
> - Narrative function: Reveal prejudice's origin: the spirit is a substitute classified as abnormal.

### S03 Dark corridor · INT. · Night ｜ [00:36–00:52]

*“It never speaks, but at night it moves so softly.”*

The camera advances along mottled walls. Old family photographs show a blurred little figure in a corner, drawn as incomplete. At the corridor's end, a half-open door releases blue-green light like mist.

The spirit stands motionless inside, hair trembling, fingertips luminous like dawn dew on grass.

    CARETAKER (murmuring): “It… isn't human. I shouldn't look.”

He reaches to shut the door. His fingers stop in midair.

Sound: Wind-light footsteps; luminous vibration like a heartbeat.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Long advancing take with side rotation → sudden freeze on spirit.
> - Composition: Low angle, spirit upper left, door focal, people at visual margins.
> - Lighting: Soft blue-green side backlight against yellow interior; blurred shadows.
> - Color: Cyan and yellow, blue-gray and iron-yellow dominant; unresolved.
> - Atmosphere: Tension and uncertainty, medium intensity.
> - Music: Light piano touch, clearer timbre like wind at a window.
> - Transition: In—drifting light; out—spirit's shadow in gap.
> - Narrative function: Establish its existence in opposition to prejudice.

### S04 The seventh door · INT. · Dusk ｜ [00:53–01:15]

*“The door won't open. Only when you're willing will it creak.”*

In the cellar corner stands the seventh door: “Not human, not alien, not to be expelled.” The caretaker touches it. Its grain does not move.

He sits and closes his eyes. After a moment, the gap trembles and blue-green light seeps in.

    CARETAKER (almost inaudible): “You… aren't a monster.”

He slowly looks up. The spirit sits beside the door, brows lowered, fingertip light pulsing like stars.

Sound: Trembling gap, wind across cellar stone, clear drops.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed → slow handheld push → pull out to light in the gap.
> - Composition: Eye level, door centered, spirit lower left, symmetrical light.
> - Lighting: Soft oblique natural cyan, low contrast; calm.
> - Color: Desaturated cool cyan, blue-gray and white; easing emotion.
> - Atmosphere: Quiet with a little hope, low intensity.
> - Music: Simplified piano, single-note breathing loop.
> - Transition: In—door glimmer; out—glowing fingertips.
> - Narrative function: First challenge to prejudice, an entrance to acceptance.

### S05 The spirit's morning dew · INT. · Morning ｜ [01:16–01:30]

*“I never speak, because words are knives. I only remember that people lower their heads when wind comes.”*

Slanting morning sunlight reaches the cellar corner. The spirit sits on old cloth, fingertip light falling like dew. No human shadow in the doorway, only stillness.

The camera inches forward. Dew falls from its hair and stains the cloth, like a forgotten tear.

Sound: Extremely clear drops, receding wind, only faint cloth sounds remaining.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed → slight fingertip push → falling-water close-up.
> - Composition: Overhead centered spirit, watermarks extend the dripping motif.
> - Lighting: Warm morning side backlight, translucent cyan; tender.
> - Color: Pale cyan and gold, blue-white dominant; calm.
> - Atmosphere: Low-intensity tenderness, a dream's remaining warmth.
> - Music: Piano develops, soft wooden flute like wind through woods.
> - Transition: In—drop lands; out—sun enters front hall.
> - Narrative function: Establish gentle existence and complete identification.

---

### S06 A door opens with the wind · INT. · First morning light ｜ [01:31–01:45]

*“At last I know acceptance isn't forgetting prejudice. It's seeing it, then gently putting it down.”*

Before the door, the caretaker's hands tremble. Wind enters and the bell sounds like a sigh.

He slowly touches the frame. The door opens by itself, light spilling out. The spirit stands motionless, watching him.

    CARETAKER (calm, soft): “The seventh door… Today, I opened it.”

The camera rises. Sunlight fills the hall. His back stands in the light; his shadow and the spirit's lie side by side, symmetrical on the wall.

Sound: Wind grows, bell echoes, then quiet with wind alone.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Static → slow push toward caretaker → rise and pull to full hall.
> - Composition: Medium-wide, symmetrical person and door; spirit lower right mirrors its shadow.
> - Lighting: Soft direct warm daylight, extremely low contrast; soothing.
> - Color: Warm yellow and blue-white, gold and blue-gray dominant; fulfillment.
> - Atmosphere: Extremely gentle, low intensity, lasting resonance.
> - Music: Piano closes, wooden flute recedes, spacious final tones.
> - Transition: In—bell; out—sunlight and steady doorway glow.
> - Narrative function: Close the theme: prejudice understood rather than eliminated; a warm ending.
