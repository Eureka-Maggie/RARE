# Short screenplay: The Suffocation Checklist of Perfect Genes
**Genre:** Social satire / science-fiction fable / suspenseful realism  
**Duration:** Approximately 7 minutes  
**Plot points:** 6  
**Directorial sensibility:** Olivier Assayas + the quiet observation of *Shoplifters*

## Overall tone

- **Central theme:** Once perfect genes become society's standard, people must suppress their flaws. So-called normality is an institution sustaining collective suffocation.
- **Visual style:** Cool minimalism, realistic live-action details, facial microexpressions contrasted with rigid bodies.
- **Narrative technique:** Nonlinear slices of time follow a defect-specimen log, collaging past and present memories.
- **Perspective:** Detached third-person observation through a genetic archivist; apparent neutrality conceals critical irony.
- **Music:** Low synthesizer pulses in the style of *Black Mirror*, with mechanical beats increasing year by year; calm develops into oppression.
- **Emotional arc:** Calm → doubt → detached observation → sudden loss of control → awareness of suffocation → final silence.
- **Special devices:** The defect repository recurs through mirrors, reflections, and projections as an erased self. Every smile is slightly displaced and twitching, suggesting physical discomfort. Numbered “real genetic test reports” increase sequentially without mortality or disease evidence.

## Screenplay

### S01 Genetic archive · INT. · Day ｜ [00:00–00:18]

*“Every defect is marked ‘negligible.’ We aren't treating disease. We're confirming the boundaries of a collective fantasy.”*

In the glass corridor, Lin Yan runs a fingertip over file box 001. He looks up. His reflection in the distant mirror twitches, as though briefly touched. He does not turn around.

    LIN YAN (calm, slightly hesitant): “Specimen 001: abnormal threshold for emotional fluctuations. File under ‘mild maladaptation.’ Recommend routine family intervention.”

*“Sometimes I wonder whether we're screening children, or scanning the part of ourselves afraid of reality.”*

Sound: Sliding metal, low thermostat hum, distant children's laughter deliberately filtered to retain only its first half.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed → slow push toward box → side-mirror insert facing the protagonist.
> - Composition: Low eye level → frontal medium → overhead reflection.
> - Lighting: Cold white top light, uneven at dark card edges; low contrast, oppressive.
> - Color: 4500K, desaturated silver-gray and gray-blue; calm, detached.
> - Atmosphere: Oppressive order, an institutional log with no participants.
> - Music: Low electronic pulses in a steady, breathing three-beat loop.
> - Transition: In—emerge from a cracked glass reflection; out—rotating cut with children's laughter.
> - Narrative function: Establish the world, defects, and institutional indifference.

### S02 Family screening day · INT. · Morning ｜ [00:19–00:42]

*“The adults smile. The child closes their eyes. They don't know that the contracting muscles are the body's resistance to a fundamental incompatibility.”*

Zhou Wan puts new shoes on the child while her husband studies a tablet. The child's eyes are tightly shut. Everyone smiles, with brief, quiet laughter whose endings seem cut off.

    ZHOU WAN (sweetly smiling, throat tightening): “Sweetheart, be like perfect genes. Grow into a flower that will never crack.”

*“They cannot see how the corners of the child's smile suddenly sink, as if something were sucking them down.”*

Sound: Faint persistent infant coughing; family ambience edited to retain only pleasant parts; outdoor air-conditioning cut into intermittent white noise.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld tracking → fixed medium → quick cut as the child's eyes close, then barely open.
> - Composition: Medium → low angle on parents → extreme close-up of trembling fingers.
> - Lighting: Even front-side light, tiny unnatural facial shadows, geometrically cut light patches.
> - Color: Warmish 5500K, extremely low saturation; plastic gray-white clothes, false emotion.
> - Atmosphere: Apparent harmony concealing tense physical discomfort.
> - Music: Cheerful electronic strings accelerate like a rising happiness index.
> - Transition: In—quivering eyelashes; out—darken to “Defect specimen: 002.”
> - Narrative function: Show the disguise of perfect life; foreshadow bodily discomfort.

---

### S03 Defect repository (flashback) · INT. · Night ｜ [00:43–01:05]

*“We call defects errors, but errors are never random. They inhabit every breath's rhythm and every delayed heartbeat.”*

Lin Yan opens cabinet 009. Its door slides open automatically, exposing long-inactive specimens labeled “Temporary archive. Emotional intervention recommended.” He stops and addresses the corner camera.

    LIN YAN (almost to himself): “Number 009, emotional aggression threshold: 0.83. Treatment recommendation: three seconds of smile training daily.”

*“We aren't healing anyone. We're teaching everyone how not to express anger when they're angry.”*

Sound: Faint refrigeration, metal doors, electrical current like breathing.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slow push → scan beam across labels → close-up of yellowing “smile training” label.
> - Composition: Low angle on cabinet → overhead label → cracked ink of “training” in extreme close-up.
> - Lighting: Bluish cold and floating ambient light; dim specimen highlights suggest absent life.
> - Color: Cold-blue 4000K, gray-white; red appears only in label text; oppressive.
> - Atmosphere: Reality within absurdity, a record of institutional forgetting.
> - Music: Electronic pulse suddenly slows half a beat, creating delay.
> - Transition: In—a label slips away, leaving blankness; out—black screen and drips.
> - Narrative function: Reveal institutional defect handling and prepare Chen Mo's introduction.

---

### S04 Chen Mo's apartment · INT. · Night ｜ [01:06–01:38]

*“I stopped knowing whether I have a defect long ago. I only know I shake when it gets cold—not from cold, but because I cannot control how often I breathe.”*

Chen Mo reads yellowed pages by the window, unconsciously pressing them with his fingers. Each page makes his lips tremble. He looks up at faintly flickering neon, his eyes empty.

    CHEN MO (low, hoarse, almost whispering): “They say genes determine fate. But fate is only somebody else's definition of a comfort zone.”

*“I haven't done anything wrong. I'm just… too real.”*

Sound: Air-conditioner hum, unedited traffic outside, slow and broken page turns.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed → slow handheld movement → slow-motion trembling fingers.
> - Composition: Oblique medium → window silhouette → extreme lip close-up.
> - Lighting: Yellow interior contrasts with cold blue outside; overlapping window light forms a crack.
> - Color: Warm yellow inside, cold blue outside, gray-yellow dominant; exhausted clarity.
> - Atmosphere: Authenticity and loneliness; his movements contrast with the setting.
> - Music: Melodiless low electrical sound, like an interrupted heartbeat.
> - Transition: In—“009” appears in reflected neon; out—move to the AI screen.
> - Narrative function: Introduce a real human being against the perfect specimens.

---

### S05 AI surveillance log · INT. · Day ｜ [01:39–02:05]

*“The system never feels anxious. Humans do. A defect is never one person's failure; it is society's systemic imbalance.”*

The control-room screen lights up: “Unreported defects: 1,736.” A list updates beneath it. Lin Yan clicks specimen 009. A prompt appears:

> “Recommended intervention: synchronize emotional and bodily rhythms. Initiate family intervention plan.”

    ZHAO NIAN (steady AI voice): “Specimen 009 synchronized to family intervention module. Family members should conduct three seconds of smile training daily.”

*“They don't know we aren't correcting life. We're teaching everyone to stop breathing.”*

Sound: Fluorescent lights switching on, screen refreshing, clear but unvarying AI voice.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Wide angle → slow screen push → close-up of the smile-training button being pressed.
> - Composition: Overhead interface → medium AI voice box → enlarge the texture of “smile.”
> - Lighting: Strong natural light, segmented illumination, cold interface reflections.
> - Color: Natural white with fluorescent blue-green interfaces; chilling.
> - Atmosphere: Cold, warmthless institution; an automated trial.
> - Music: Steady and emotionless, perfectly synchronized with the voice.
> - Transition: In—system pop-up; out—freeze-frame, rain begins.
> - Narrative function: Complete the satirical cycle of systematic defect processing.

---

### S06 Final archive scan · INT. · Dusk ｜ [02:06–02:30]

*“Every smile is complete. Every defect is filed. Every truth has become a standardized gesture.”*

Everyone stands in the main hall, movements synchronized under gentle lights, smiling. Lin Yan presses Scan. “Archive completeness: 100%.” Looking into the camera, he suddenly speaks softly.

    LIN YAN (almost murmuring): “We've finally perfected the beauty of genes, and forgotten that people breathe.”

The image darkens. One last line appears:

> “Notice: All defect specimens have been archived as family intervention cases. Search and access prohibited.”

*“Perfection is the institution's endpoint. Authenticity is humanity's beginning.”*

Sound: Laughter entirely edited away except for a faint echo; extreme silence, air-conditioning continuing.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Wide pullback → push toward crowd → slow-motion freeze of each smile → slow push to Lin Yan's face.
> - Composition: Medium group shot → overhead → close-up of reddened eyes.
> - Lighting: Warm dusk, darkened faces create shadows inside smiles.
> - Color: Warm-yellow 5500K, extremely low saturation; plastic smiles, oppressive.
> - Atmosphere: Utterly false harmony, the audience's awareness completely numbed.
> - Music: Background stops; only the final second of a fading electronic pulse.
> - Transition: In—smiling crowd; out—black screen, “Defect 009” slowly appears, unscanned.
> - Narrative function: Climax and resolution reveal perfection's suffocating nature, with a lasting aftereffect.

**— End —**
