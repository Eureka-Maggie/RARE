# Short screenplay: Tree Language
**Genre:** Nature fable / absurd realism / philosophical reflection / elegy  
**Duration:** Approximately 6 minutes  
**Plot points:** 6  
**Directorial sensibility:** Hirokazu Kore-eda × George Santayana

---

## Overall tone

- **Central theme:** Approaching nature in the name of ownership quietly strips living things of the dignity that belongs to time and change.
- **Visual style:** Cool film texture, slight fading, minimalist compositions, and slowly advancing plant growth.
- **Narrative technique:** Linear progression interspersed with memories and a parallel account of natural growth. A tree's life cycle becomes a metaphor for the absurdity and cost of human possession.
- **Perspective:** First-person interior narration by a young botanist witnessing a tree's death and rebirth.
- **Music:** Airy guqin, gradually joined by faint wind chimes; a breathing pace, with detached electronic pulses later imitating mechanical timekeeping.
- **Emotional arc:** Calm → doubt → fear → remorse → silence → lingering resonance.
- **Special devices:**
  - Reversed images interweave growth and withering as visual irony.
  - A carved mark recurs at different angles and times as foreshadowing.
  - All dialogue is whispered, gradually diminishing toward the volume of wind.

## Characters

| Name | Identity | Appearance | Personality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lin Yuan | Young botanist | 28; gray-white shirt with frayed cuffs, an old metal clip on his left ear, a faint scar on his right shoulder | Reflective and restrained; draws tree cross-sections in a notebook and never looks up at people when speaking |
| Tree (unnamed) | Natural entity | No definite form; conveyed through light, shadow, and the movement of branches; every frame seems to breathe | Unconscious, yet expresses a will to exist through growth and fracture |

## Screenplay

### S01 The first tree shadow · INT. · Day / dusk ｜ [00:00–00:45]

*“The first time I saw the tree's shadow, it wasn't on the hillside. It was in the corner of a wall, like a dream just waking up.”*

The camera slowly rises from the ground. The shadow writhes in a crack, breathing; blue-silver moisture shines at the leaf edges. Lin Yuan stands outside the fence, his elongated silhouette overlapping the swaying tree shadow. He raises a hand and brushes the corner with his fingertips. A faint friction sounds in the air.

    LIN YUAN (whispering, uncertain): “It's moving… It isn't the wind. They're talking.”

Sound: Leaves rustling, distant mountain-stream droplets ticking, an occasional birdsong cut short.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Stationary push-in → tiny pans, 0.5 seconds each.
> - Composition: Low angle; tree shadow fills the lower left, elongating the figure.
> - Lighting: Oblique sidelight, cool gray-blue, small patches, low contrast; quiet but oppressive.
> - Color: 4500K; 30% saturation; silver-gray and pale blue; mistlike feeling.
> - Atmosphere: A latent threat in silence; medium intensity.
> - Music: Solo guqin, slowly rising harmonics, extremely slow rhythm.
> - Transition: In—windblown leaves enter frame; out—low angle becomes eye level.
> - Narrative function: Establish the tree as an unfamiliar presence and the mystery of its movement.

### S02 The first mark · INT. · Day ｜ [00:46–01:20]

*“I asked myself who could have carved that number in 2013. I hadn't even been born then.”*

Lin Yuan stands inside the wooden door, a hand against the wall. Wind moves his metal ear clip with a faint chime. The “2013” carving looks rain-washed and blurred. As he touches its edge, the mark shifts slightly, like something alive breathing.

    LIN YUAN (to himself, fingers trembling): “It remembers me being here before… But I don't remember coming.”

Sound: A slight iron-door creak, rust scraping, wind ruffling pages, a distant pump dripping.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed long take with slight handheld shake to suggest displaced memory.
> - Composition: Frontal; carving centered; light divides his left side, while the right side is empty.
> - Lighting: Strong front-side light divides light and dark, with faint reflection at shadow edges; suspenseful.
> - Color: 4800K; 40% saturation; iron-gray and light brown; oppressive.
> - Atmosphere: Distorted memory; high intensity.
> - Music: Guqin changes key, its rhythm dragging slightly, with faint electronic pulses.
> - Transition: In—enlarge the instant fingers touch the mark, revealing texture; out—cut to Lin Yuan walking away.
> - Narrative function: Introduce possession's first trace: leaving a human mark on nature implies encroachment.

### S03 A promise beneath the canopy · INT. · Afternoon ｜ [01:21–02:05]

*“When I reached out to touch it, it reached back and held my hand.”*

The camera advances from Lin Yuan's perspective. He stands among trees, dappled sunlight falling through the leaves, the canopy covering him like an umbrella. Slowly, he extends his right hand and touches the trunk. The bark's patterns are as clear as growth rings. Its shadow moves toward him across the ground, as if responding.

    LIN YUAN (breathing more slowly, calm): “So it wasn't motionless. It was just waiting for me. Waiting for someone willing to believe in it.”

Sound: Leaves swishing, wind humming through gaps between trees, faint insects.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld lateral movement and tracking, leaning slightly forward.
> - Composition: Medium shot; figure and trunk on opposite sides; ground shadows form a symmetrical triangle.
> - Lighting: Natural sidelight, warm yellow, regularly spaced patches; tender yet tense.
> - Color: 5500K; 50% saturation; yellow-green and pale brown; strong trust.
> - Atmosphere: Tenderness with vigilance; medium intensity.
> - Music: Guqin melody returns with faint string harmonics.
> - Transition: In—touch leads to a slow push toward a full view of the trunk; out—cut to the crawling shadow.
> - Narrative function: Establish mutual subjecthood and empathy between human and tree; possession requires reciprocal recognition.

### S04 The artificial fracture · INT. · Night ｜ [02:06–03:10]

*“They said a saw could save a tree. But what the saw cut open was its memory.”*

Under the laboratory's pallid lights, Lin Yuan stands before the sawn-off trunk. Saw teeth gleam. The split heartwood exposes veins like broken blood vessels, yellow sap slowly seeping through the stillness. He reaches out. The heartwood moves almost imperceptibly, as though breathing.

    LIN YUAN (shaking voice, almost inaudible): “It's hurting… Like I hurt the first time my research proposal was rejected.”

Sound: Saw teeth screech; slow-motion drops fall one by one; a bulb occasionally flickers.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld shake, half-speed slow motion, stretched close-up.
> - Composition: Low angle; cross-section occupies two-thirds of the image, Lin Yuan tiny as dust below.
> - Lighting: Strong front-side cold white light, uneven and high-contrast; oppressive.
> - Color: 6500K; 55% saturation; white-yellow and pale red; cruel.
> - Atmosphere: Shock and pain; high intensity.
> - Music: Guqin stops abruptly; electronic pulses erupt like a tearing heartbeat.
> - Transition: In—slow the sawing as heartwood splits; out—black screen with dripping alone.
> - Narrative function: Reveal possession as forcibly cutting life with tools to produce an illusion of ownership.

---

### S05 Tree language returns to zero · INT. · Late night ｜ [03:11–04:00]

*“I heard it say: You took away my name. You took away the way I breathe.”*

Lin Yuan crouches by the roots. The trunk cracks open, exposing decay. Moonlight passes through the glasslike heartwood, where faint light flows like liquid starlight. Wind whispers slowly through the opening, echoing.

    LIN YUAN (voice breaking, almost voiceless): “I… I only wanted to understand it… But I turned it into an exhibit.”

Sound: Wind moaning through the split, liquid gurgling, a meaningless distant bell.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed long take with an extremely slow push, quarter speed.
> - Composition: Ground-level eye line; split centered, figure beneath it, creating a sense of being swallowed.
> - Lighting: Soft cold white moonlight from the side; extremely low contrast; sorrowful.
> - Color: 3800K; 20% saturation; gray-white and pale blue; stillness.
> - Atmosphere: Mourning and self-reproach; extremely intense.
> - Music: Guqin falls completely silent; wind remains, then electronic pulses fade into white noise.
> - Transition: In—floating heartwood light blurs into darkness; out—black screen with three seconds of wind.
> - Narrative function: Climax and moral judgment of possession; complete emotional collapse.

---

### S06 A glimmer rekindled · INT. · Dawn ｜ [04:01–04:55]

*“The new shoot has no carvings. It grows as though humans had never touched it.”*

Wind sweeps the barren mountaintop. At the stump's edge, a tender shoot slowly raises its head, its veins as clear as a first brushstroke. The camera moves closer. The tip trembles, sunlight falls, and patches of light form an unbroken arc on the ground.

    LIN YUAN (softly, almost sighing): “Perhaps nothing ever needed to be ‘owned.’ Perhaps it only needed to be ‘seen.’”

Sound: Faint rustling of new leaves, wind across the ridge, the first morning birdcall.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slightly unsteady push-in, ending in a macro freeze-frame of the shoot tip.
> - Composition: Low angle and extremely close push-in; tip level with the lens.
> - Lighting: Soft oblique warm yellow sidelight; extremely low contrast; healing.
> - Color: 5800K; 60% saturation; golden yellow and light green; regeneration.
> - Atmosphere: Peace, resonance, hope; low but far-reaching intensity.
> - Music: Guqin returns as a single melodic line, easing into its final resonance.
> - Transition: In—the shoot trembles like breathing; out—camera retreats until stump and dawn merge.
> - Narrative function: Close the cycle: the unmarked shoot shows possession is unsustainable and nature has its own order.

**— End —**
