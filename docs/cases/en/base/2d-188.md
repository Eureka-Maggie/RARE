# Short screenplay: The Glass Oath
**Genre:** Dark fairy tale / magical realism / fantasy coming-of-age / psychological drama  
**Duration:** Approximately 6 minutes  
**Plot points:** 6  
**Directorial sensibility:** Dalí × Rasputin × Nolan-like psychological suspension

## Overall tone

- **Central theme:** Love cannot be exchanged; the price is the dissolution of the soul. Certain untradeable debts of kindness ultimately consume the desire for redemption.
- **Visual style:** Cold, exquisite Eastern fantasy; a crystallized, glasslike world with translucent physical laws, reality and illusion overlapping like reflections in mist.
- **Narrative technique:** Linear progression, with turning points created through memory flashes and ruptures in reality; displaced time produces unreliability.
- **Perspective:** Limited first person, with a rift between subjective memory and reality.
- **Music:** Resonant low-frequency electronics and guqin; slow, drawn-out rhythms, glass-scraping timbres, brief bell echoes midway.
- **Emotional arc:** Calm → anxiety → determination → collapse → passive acceptance → eternal silence → lingering echoes.
- **Special devices:** Glass shards recur as memories with pale, fractured edges; displaced light and temporal echoes show the future while characters describe the past; the final sacrifice strips away both body and memory.

## Characters

| Name | Identity | Appearance | Personality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lin Yan | Former guardian, now penitent | 24; long blue-gray hair, a dull glass bead embedded in his left eye, black robe embroidered with broken vine patterns | Calm, stubborn, almost religious faith in love; habitually rubs an old pocket watch in his palm |
| Shen Li | Imprisoned lover | 22; pale skin, red lips, flowing golden gauze, glass-thread hair; refracts faint light when walking | Cool and transparent, needs no words; when she cries, the whole space freezes into frost |

## Screenplay

### S01 Crescent-moon alley · INT. · Day / night ｜ [00:00–00:30]

*“She promised that if I guarded this piece of glass, she would never be sealed away forever. But now she is no longer the person I knew.”*

Lin Yan stops at the alley entrance, his pocket watch ticking. As the hands reach midnight, the ground's reflection reveals another version of him, smiling brightly while carrying Shen Li through a blizzard—a scene he has never actually seen.

    LIN YAN (whispering, stroking the watch): “You said glass cannot be exchanged, and love cannot be bought. Then why must you die after every transaction?”

*“I once thought I could win her back by entering that mirrored hall and trading my name for her awakening.”*

Sound: Wind through cracks, intermittent distant pendulum, a faint click as the mirrored ground trembles.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed long take → close tracking of mirror reflections.
> - Composition: Low angle and symmetrical reflections.
> - Lighting: Cold white and blue-gray side backlight; phantom reflections, extremely low contrast.
> - Color: Cool blue, 70% saturation, gray-white and celadon green.
> - Atmosphere: Distance, introspection, displaced memory; medium intensity.
> - Music: Single-note guqin glissando, low pulses intensifying every eight seconds.
> - Transition: In—glide through a ground-mirror crack; out—dim and blur the mirror.
> - Narrative function: Establish gaps in his memory and foreshadow unreliable transactions.

### S02 Deep-well corridor · INT. · Night ｜ [00:31–01:15]

*“I saw a hundred ways I could die. In every one she was alive, but… her eyes had changed.”*

Lin Yan enters the corridor. Each inverted glass wall reflects another Lin Yan: embracing Shen Li, sinking underwater, burning his own name.

He stops to watch his smiling reflection reach toward hers. The real Shen Li stands far away, paper-white and silent.

    LIN YAN (trembling, barely whispering): “Can you see me? Every time I save you, I spend part of my life… But you never remember me holding you.”

*“I have to know whether she still remembers me.”*

Sound: Faint glass cracks with an “echo differential”—echoes arrive 0.3 seconds after reality.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Dolly forward → cuts among multiple reflections → frozen mirror image.
> - Composition: Diagonal crops within reflections; nearest foreground blurred.
> - Lighting: Cold white overhead light and multiple reflections create high-contrast shadows.
> - Color: Blue-violet, 80% saturation, glass purple and bronze brown.
> - Atmosphere: Confusion, overlapping memories, self-doubt; high intensity.
> - Music: Electronic bass enters, resonating discordantly with guqin; intermittent rhythm.
> - Transition: In—through a mirror gap; out—slowly withdraw through glass cracks.
> - Narrative function: Undermine the possibility of rescue and ground the reality of confused memories.

---

### S03 Glass altar · INT. · Night ｜ [01:16–02:05]

*“She lives in crystal, like light sealed in a bottle. I open the door, but she says, ‘You're too late.’”*

The altar's crystal sphere slowly turns. Shen Li's image moves inside, constantly tearing apart. Her hair becomes ash; her voice passes from right ear to left in a scrambled sequence.

Lin Yan approaches and touches the crystal. His remembered wedding suddenly runs backward: he kneels in snow while Shen Li smiles and turns away. “What we should never have exchanged was ‘forever.’”

    LIN YAN (hoarse, tearful): “You said, ‘Glass is their life, not our ransom.’ Then why do you want me to trade my life for your existence?”

*“I wanted to trade my remaining years for your life. But you already knew the exchange would turn you into an empty shell.”*

Sound: Faint crying inside the sphere, like snapping stitches; the outer floor pulses like a heartbeat.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Dolly and zoom inside the crystal → external pullback into emptiness.
> - Composition: Central symmetry, fisheye wide angle inside the sphere.
> - Lighting: Strong cold white center, dark patches from peripheral light leaks; an engulfing halo.
> - Color: Intensely cold, transparent blue and blood red, 60% saturation.
> - Atmosphere: Despair, shattered understanding, fatalism; extreme intensity.
> - Music: Electronic sound collapses, guqin reverses, one bell at the end.
> - Transition: In—enter the crystal; out—crystal shatters and white light spreads.
> - Narrative function: Reveal the true cost of exchanging love; deliver an emotional blow.

---

### S04 Trading hall · INT. · Dusk ｜ [02:06–03:20]

*“Each time I exchange a memory for her breath, she lives another day. But I move further from myself.”*

Lin Yan enters the mirrored hall. Its four walls are covered with tangible objects: a lamp, a stretch of years, a vow, a key.

He picks up “your future birthday”—his sixteenth birthday, when he said, “I will marry you.” He tries to trade it for Shen Li's consciousness, but touching it brings up a dinner with her. She smiles, saying, “You've already forgotten the sound of rain that year.”

    LIN YAN (hoarse): “I give you my entire life, and all I receive is forgetting that you once smiled.”

*“So every transaction turns love back into data.”*

Sound: Light metallic chimes and turning pages at each touch; the ground beneath him subsides slightly after each transaction.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld shake → object close-ups → quick reaction cuts.
> - Composition: Overhead, symmetrical, evenly spaced objects suggest an altar.
> - Lighting: High oblique top light casts long shadows; softly darkened object edges.
> - Color: Gold-orange over cold gray, gradually shifting from warm to cold.
> - Atmosphere: Exhaustion, absurdity, systematic indifference; medium-high intensity.
> - Music: Repeated mechanical phrase; each transaction lengthens its rhythm by 0.5 seconds.
> - Transition: In—push along touching fingers; out—the whole image retreats as if compressed.
> - Narrative function: Reveal that love cannot be traded and develop his collapse in understanding.

---

### S05 The oath in the mirror · INT. · Night ｜ [03:21–04:30]

*“I entered and found time running backward. There, I had never met her and had never loved her.”*

Lin Yan falls through the crack into the world of the mirror oath. Time reverses. He sees himself at ten, Shen Li watching and smiling: “If you don't understand me, don't come near me.”

He opens his mouth to shout, but no sound comes. The mirrored world steadily plays on: every encounter with Shen Li occurs before he has ever met her.

He finally understands: “saving her” merely compensates for her being forgotten within time.

    LIN YAN (calm, empty-eyed): “So I never truly possessed her. I only invented a gentle version of myself inside her memories.”

*“I cannot save her, because I never truly loved her.”*

Sound: Reversed bells, a heartbeat gradually stilling, finally only a drop falling into emptiness.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Circling tracking shot → rapid reverse playback → freeze-frame.
> - Composition: Orbit the central axis; reflected figures create mirror symmetry.
> - Lighting: Unlit center, mistlike cold edges; visual collapse of time.
> - Color: Black with faint blue, 10% saturation, dominant void-white.
> - Atmosphere: Emptiness, fate, dismantling the self; extreme intensity.
> - Music: One second of complete silence, then an extremely faint bell.
> - Transition: In—fall through the crack; out—the mirror closes abruptly into black.
> - Narrative function: Complete the reversal of his understanding; reveal that the love did not actually exist.

---

### S06 The end of silence · EXT. · Morning ｜ [04:31–05:50]

*“She isn't beside me, yet every morning I see her shadow. Is it memory, or an illusion?”*

Lin Yan stands outside the tower where Shen Li once was. In faint dawn light, its summit is empty. A broken piece of glass lies on the ground, reflecting his blurred face.

He crouches and gently picks it up. It cracks with the faintest click.

No sound, no response. Only wind passing like breath.

    LIN YAN (softly, almost inaudible): “I thought that if I won, you would return. But winning was never the answer. Love is losing and finding again; knowing there is no hope, and still choosing to come closer.”

*“Now I understand. She wasn't sealed away. She never left. I simply forgot how to truly see her.”*

Sound: Wind, shattering glass, a very distant bell that does not ring but merely exists.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slow push-in → pullback → fixed long take.
> - Composition: Low angle; protagonist and glass form a triangle against emptiness.
> - Lighting: Soft, natural warm-orange morning light from the side.
> - Color: Warm orange and gray-white, 40% saturation, dominant late-morning sky blue.
> - Atmosphere: Quiet, resonance, compassion; low but persistent intensity.
> - Music: One guqin note slowly rises, ending in soundless emptiness.
> - Transition: In—camera slowly opens the view; out—glass breaks, image goes black.
> - Narrative function: Conclude in silence; tragedy is existence itself, not merely an ending.

**— End —**
