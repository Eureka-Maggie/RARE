# Short screenplay: Lingering Warmth in an Empty Alley
**Genre**: Realism / Neighborhood survival / Marginalized youth / Emotional documentary  
**Duration**: Approximately 6 minutes  
**Plot points**: 6  
**Directorial sensibility**: The Dardenne brothers × Shin Seung-hun: handheld documentary, poor-neighborhood texture, collapse through a small incident

---

## Overall tone
- **Central idea**: A tiny conflict grows through the gaps of daily life in a poor neighborhood, leaving no solution, only worn reality and empty streets.
- **Visual style**: Handheld, low-angle tracking, authentic clutter, gray tones with local highlights on old wall tiles and rain stains.
- **Narrative approach**: Linear: daily life → friction → loss of control → exit. Details replace explanations; process matters more than motive.
- **Point of view**: Limited first person through Xiao Dong, observing and sensing without emotional judgment.
- **Music**: Mainly low ambient sound, no melodic score. Traffic, noisy children, and metal buckets provide breathing rhythm, fading to silence at the end.
- **Emotional arc**: Calm → minor friction → tense confrontation → angry eruption → emotional paralysis → empty ending.
- **Devices**:
  1. Slight handheld shake throughout simulates uncontrolled street documentary.
  2. Repeated touches of a wall nail and looks at worn soles foreshadow the saved half-piece of candy.
  3. Rough, unadorned dialogue uses short sentences and dialect, reflecting street speech.
  4. No solution: conflict unresolved, people remain, social rules unchallenged; only still surroundings remain.

## Characters
| Name | Identity | Appearance | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiao Dong | 17, marginalized neighborhood youth; left school to work | Faded camouflage work trousers, old torn T-shirt, left shoe sole worn through | Calm surface, sensitive underneath; erupts when ignored, notices surroundings, speaks hesitantly and defensively |
| A Biao | 20, Xiao Dong's peer, a neighborhood delinquent | Leather jacket zipped to the top, finger scars, often a cigarette in his mouth | Seems impulsive but controls situations; harsh speech without deep feeling, disregards rules |
| Old Wang | 50, neighborhood scrap collector | Tall and thin, gray cloth clothes, slightly stooped, dangling left earring | Low voice, avoids conflict; tired, observing eyes; the neighborhood's silent witness |

## Screenplay

### S01: Waiting at the corner · INT. · Day ｜ [00:00–00:45]

*“Every day before noon I come around to check whether that nail's loose. Not for the nail. I'm afraid it'll fall on someone's foot.”*

Xiao Dong crouches behind the abandoned shack, knees pressed to concrete, left hand unconsciously stroking a rusty nail. Green corrosion, cracked edges. He looks at a long thin worn mark on his left sole, as if repeatedly scraped by something hard.

Children kick a plastic bottle at the alley entrance, laughing amid distant bicycles. Slanting sunlight stretches a narrow wall-corner shadow across half the alley.

A Biao approaches with half a cigarette, pointing. “Nobody touches this place.”

*“Touches? Nobody said anything about touching. This corner, this nail—nobody's moved it for a year.”*

Sound: wind, brakes, plastic bottle thumps and creaks, distant dog.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld stabilizer, slight shake at 0.5 mm/second, following his gaze.
> - Composition: Ground-level low angle; shadow covers his face except the left half and eyes.
> - Lighting: High-contrast natural sidelight; backlit nail, deep corner shadow, indistinct figures.
> - Color: Cold gray, rust-brown and concrete gray, dark-green nail highlights, low saturation.
> - Atmosphere: Calm but oppressive, strongly real; viewers sense an unsafe place.
> - Music: Traffic, wind, children; low ambient sound, no melodic score.
> - Transition: Naturally push from corner shadow; tilt up into the main alley.
> - Narrative function: Establish place and routine, foreshadow nail and worn sole.

### S02: Smoking by the broken wall · INT. · Day ｜ [00:46–01:20]

*“He never talks. But whenever he smokes, I know something's about to change.”*

Xiao Dong sits on an old wooden chair beside the shack, half a bottle of water at his feet. Opposite, A Biao swings a cigarette end, faintly smiling. About 30 centimeters separate them. Xiao Dong keeps looking at his shoes; A Biao at wall cracks.

“Candy stand's raising prices at lunch,” A Biao says flatly. “Two-fifty.”

Without looking up: “You owed me one-thirty last time. I paid it back to you.”

“That was before. Old debts don't count now.” A Biao sneers, grinding the butt into a metal bucket, sparks scattering. “You asked me why I didn't ask for more?”

Quietly: “I asked. You only said, ‘Give it time.’”

A Biao glances at him and mechanically rolls another cigarette. Wind through the shack sends paper scraps circling. Sunlight cuts diagonally between them, their shadows displaced.

Xiao Dong looks toward the nail, fingers unconsciously rubbing his worn sole.

Sound: cigarette friction, rustling paper, creaking metal, distant children's giggles.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld push from the side into opposing positions, continually tracking with slight sway.
> - Composition: Diagonal, A Biao right, Xiao Dong left, space between; foreground nail obscured.
> - Lighting: Natural slanting light illuminates faces differently; real shadows in dark areas.
> - Color: Desaturated gray-blue, gray-white smoke, cold reflections from the bucket.
> - Atmosphere: Calm surface, hidden tension; brief opposing statements without an outburst.
> - Music: Continuous ambience; children fade, traffic rises and settles.
> - Transition: Enter on exhaled smoke; move right as Xiao Dong studies his shoe, then slowly withdraw to the corner.
> - Narrative function: Establish the relationship and price conflict, setting the next trigger.

### S03: Spilled drink · INT. · Day ｜ [01:21–02:10]

*“All I wanted was half a piece of candy. Then he moved, and the bottle went over.”*

Xiao Dong approaches the stand. Three drink bottles sit on a metal cabinet, a plastic tag reading “2.5 yuan.” “Half a piece. I'll take it away.”

A Biao searches a recycling bin for old bags. Without looking up: “Costs money. Stop staring at the candy.”

Xiao Dong reaches for candy, slips, and stumbles forward. He instinctively grabs the iron frame, but A Biao suddenly pushes him.

A bottle clatters down. Orange liquid splashes over concrete, plastic stool, and price tag, spreading in an irregular circle. His shoe is instantly wet. He staggers half a step back.

A Biao turns with a sneer. “Trying to take advantage again?”

Crouching, Xiao Dong wipes his heel with his shirt. Hoarsely: “I just wanted candy.”

A Biao advances. “No half pieces. Whole or nothing.”

They remain locked in place. Xiao Dong keeps touching his sole, already softening.

Sound: bottle clang, fizzing and splashing liquid, creaking stool, stumbling footsteps.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Rapidly approach his fall, then level shot; slow motion on spreading liquid.
> - Composition: Tracking medium shot, spreading liquid foregrounded, shoe sole emphasized.
> - Lighting: Oppressive backlight; droplets create highlights, wet ground lengthens shadows.
> - Color: Orange-red liquid clashes with gray surroundings; wet reflections yellow-green.
> - Atmosphere: Sudden conflict and emotional turn; violence comes through action, not words.
> - Music: Background drops 10 dB, amplified splashing, then volume returns without rhythm.
> - Transition: Enter through the spill, slowly approach his wiping shoe; quick cut to him meeting A Biao's gaze.
> - Narrative function: Ignite the conflict over price and transaction legitimacy, escalating it and triggering emotional recoil.

---

### S04: Escalation · INT. · Day ｜ [02:11–03:40]

*“I'm not stealing. I just want to buy something normally.”*

Xiao Dong grabs and yanks the iron frame. It snaps loose; a basket overturns, scattering candy. Tightly: “You shove me. Why shouldn't I fight back?”

A Biao roars: “You fucking saying nobody at home keeps you in line?”

They collide. Xiao Dong grabs a discarded iron bar and swings at A Biao's shoulder. A Biao retreats two steps; the bar strikes the metal wall with a piercing clang.

Furious, A Biao climbs onto the shack roof, throws a cigarette butt, and sets a plastic bag in the corner alight.

Xiao Dong charges, sees flames darting through plastic, then panics and kicks over an iron bucket. It breaks, scrap rolls out. He looks down: his sole is soaked, white, soft.

He stops and does not pursue.

Old Wang slowly arrives with a worn cloth bag, stands between them, looking at the fire. “Stop lighting it. Burn it and nobody'll collect it.”

A Biao sneers back. “Collect? Who collects this place?”

Old Wang silently sets down the bag, pats it gently, and walks away, dusty trouser cuffs scraping the ground.

Sound: iron-bar impact, crackling and sizzling plastic, breaking glass, Old Wang's footsteps.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Dynamic, frequently shaking handheld; quick cuts during pursuit, slow push and hold on fire.
> - Composition: Multiple medium-close angles emphasize the bar; fire against the shack, faces obscured.
> - Lighting: Strong light through flame creates moving highlights; background shadows suddenly deepen.
> - Color: Orange-red fire against cold gray, mottled grime, darkened skin.
> - Atmosphere: Intense confrontation without a winner; violence has no consequences, the neighborhood answers silently.
> - Music: Ambient sound surges, fire amplified, layers overlap; music disappears entirely.
> - Transition: Enter on the bar hitting the wall; move past flame to Old Wang receding.
> - Narrative function: Complete loss of control, irrational action and failed community mechanisms; fire becomes symbolic destruction.

### S05: Empty lot · INT. · Day ｜ [03:41–05:00]

*“Now I don't seem to want anything.”*

Alone in the lot, Xiao Dong stands among tires and plastic buckets. Wind lifts a black cloth, delayed in its movement. He crouches and kicks over an old plastic bucket containing brittle candy wrappers and half a piece of candy.

He picks up a wrapper. “Who bought this?”

No reply. No distant pedestrians.

At a corner he touches a rusty iron bucket engraved “1989.” Cold fingertips against metal. Looking up, he sees A Biao emerging from a neighboring ruin, silently watching from a distance.

Xiao Dong starts to leave, then stops and looks down. Wear has spread from his sole to his ankle, like an edge repeatedly rubbed away.

He bites the half-piece of candy. Bitter; its wrapper is moldy. He throws it into the empty bucket and returns to the alley entrance.

A Biao does not move. Slanting city sunlight flows across the bucket as though slowly fading.

Sound: wind through tires, footsteps, cracking wrapper, distant bark.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slight handheld advance, slow approach to bucket and sole, long take following his retreat.
> - Composition: Wide overhead view makes him small and isolated; bucket and sole are focal points.
> - Lighting: Low backlit sun, points reflected from metal, elongated figures and ground-covering shadows.
> - Color: Afternoon orange stained ochre by rain and dust; dark-gray wear, faded dark-brown wrapper.
> - Atmosphere: Quiet emptiness and estrangement; unresolved conflict leads inward.
> - Music: Ambient sound returns to baseline, dominated by slow wind; no score.
> - Transition: Enter on the kicked bucket; withdraw as he disappears at the alley entrance.
> - Narrative function: Emotional collapse and self-negation; neighborhood reality runs idle, conflict falls silent.

---

### S06: Silent exit · INT. · Day ｜ [05:01–06:00]

*“I can't even remember the way home.”*

At the alley entrance Xiao Dong studies his shoes. His left sole splits, showing white, foul-smelling inner yarn. He stays, absent-mindedly picking at the wall nail.

Old Wang emerges from his old scrap vehicle and offers an iron bucket. “Maybe take this? Trade it for a new sole?”

Xiao Dong looks up, empty-eyed. “I don't want it.”

Old Wang turns without a word. Wheels creak across the ground as he slowly drives away.

Sunlight thins. Its last strands pass through the shack onto Xiao Dong's face like an old photograph. He slowly raises a hand, wipes his face, lowers it.

The camera rises from alley to entire street: cluttered, derelict, unmaintained. The nail remains, but nobody touches it now.

The wind stops. The distant city quiets.

Sound: wind ending, receding wheel creak, faint friction on the nail, city sound fading to zero.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Slowly rising long take with slight handheld sway, ending in a wide overhead shot.
> - Composition: Low view of Xiao Dong, foreground nail, Old Wang's back distant.
> - Lighting: Slanting sunset, soft but pale throughout, faint nail reflection.
> - Color: Warm gold becomes gray-white, nail dark brown, skin gray, overall defocused.
> - Atmosphere: Emptiness, forgetting, disconnection; no personal goal or possibility of remaking the surroundings.
> - Music: Background drops to zero, only the last wheel creak and dying wind remain.
> - Transition: Enter on his wiping face; withdraw to an empty street and fade.
> - Narrative function: Resolve the film's emotion without solving anything. People resume daily life; social structures silently restart.
