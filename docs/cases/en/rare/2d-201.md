# Short screenplay: The Perfect Gene Program
**Genre:** Science fiction / satire / social fable / biopower  
**Duration:** Approximately 6 minutes  
**Plot points:** 6  
**Directorial sensibility:** David Lynch × Félix González-Lynch; an aesthetic of sensory and systemic oppression

## Overall tone

- **Central theme:** When genetic perfection determines social admission, individuals must hide defects to survive. Beneath polished appearances lie psychological suffocation and the complete deprivation of authentic life.
- **Visual style:** Cool technological neon, surrealism mixed with medical-industrial aesthetics, mirrors, data streams, transparent biological structures; a distorted fusion of biochemical laboratory and elegant future home.
- **Narrative technique:** Linear screening → beautification → surveillance → inspection → collapse → afterecho, progressively exposing systemic violence.
- **Perspective:** Omniscient third person, conveying oppression through subjective breath, skin sensations, and neural signals.
- **Music:** Repetitive synthesized tracks, low biological waves layered with algorithmic rhythms, slight electronic distortion and dissonance; slow, persistent, oppressive as breathing.
- **Emotional arc:** Calm → obedience → latent anxiety → sudden loss of control → emotional collapse → a silent cry in the aftermath.
- **Special devices:** Breathlessness, stiffness, accents, and emotional changes recur as coded “risk genes.” Data erasure and transparent-body transitions signify covered-up flaws. Perfect-portrait comparisons equate outward perfection with inner suffocation. Every suppressed physical response rebounds as numb fingers, coughing, or purple skin.

## Screenplay

### S01 Initial family genetic screening · INT. · Day ｜ [00:00–00:45]

*“I never thought I would live just to breathe.”*

Lin Yao sits in the living room beside an all-in-one genetic self-test machine. One deep breath triggers a scan of facial muscle tremors, skin microcirculation, and blinking frequency. Her left brow rises 0.3 millimeters. A red warning immediately appears: “Facial asymmetry, risk level 2. Corrective aid recommended.” She blinks and presses her brow flat. On screen, a perfect genetic model resembles her almost exactly, but with a perfect jawline, peaceful eyes, and smooth breathing. She gives a faint laugh, touching the skin on the other side—glass-smooth and poreless.

    WANG LI (calm, digitally tinged): “Lin Yao, your genetic match is currently 98.7%. Complete emotion-regulation training within three days to avoid enhanced monitoring.”

Sound: Mechanical inhalation, scanning light points, a soft voice: “Heart rate stable. Breathing rhythm normal.”

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Close-up → medium shot → push toward facial scanner.
> - Composition: Overhead and eye level; subcutaneous tissue as translucent data streams.
> - Lighting: Cold white with even fill, shadowless, emphasizing flawlessness.
> - Color: 5600K, low saturation, gray-white and pale blue; oppressed compliance.
> - Atmosphere: Control, precision, mechanical order; oppression through bodily detail.
> - Music: Low electronic pulses with slight resonance; steady but oppressive.
> - Transition: In—upgraded scan interface; out—slide into living-room backdrop.
> - Narrative function: Establish the institution and immediate detection and suppression of defects.

### S02 Emotion-regulation room · INT. · Day ｜ [00:45–01:30]

*“Every time I try to smile, it feels like tearing my chest apart.”*

Infrared scanners tightly surround Lin Yao at a mirror. She tries to smile, but her mouth will not rise. “Emotional fluctuation index up 83%.” A red prompt follows: “Severely nonstandard expression. Immediate breath restoration recommended.” She closes her eyes, inhales abdominally for three seconds, and exhales slowly. In the mirror, faint ripples appear over her left lung. The system classifies them as “abnormal pulmonary expansion,” a “potential developmental defect.” She repeats the exercise, each breath matching the music: fast—slow—stop—heavy. At last a little smile appears, but her mouth is rigid. “Compulsive smile detected. Discontinue facial-muscle use.” Gasping, she feels something inside her chest breaking apart.

Sound: Breathing beat; prompts: “78% complete,” “Improve emotional control,” “Anomaly detected.”

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Multiple surveillance angles, slow-motion breath capture.
> - Composition: Mirror symmetry and continuous dissolves; body overlaid with data.
> - Lighting: Even cold light; data covers the face frame by frame, producing ripples.
> - Color: 5400K, desaturated white-blue; suffocation under behavioral control.
> - Atmosphere: Anxiety, mechanical intervention, growing bodily loss of control.
> - Music: Stronger rhythm, one breath per beat, slight electronic distortion.
> - Transition: In—data pop-up; out—hand tremor disappears.
> - Narrative function: Establish emotion as defect; provoke physical discomfort.

### S03 Blood-sample examination · INT. · Dusk ｜ [01:30–02:15]

*“They call it a health check. It's really a body audit.”*

Lin Yao is wheeled into the laboratory. Her hand is restrained and her arm inserted into a whole-genome scanning tube. A blood sample drops in; the machine analyzes its flow. Second-by-second oxygen curves appear: “Weak blood supply to left cerebral region, fluctuation 12%. Capacity-expansion training recommended.” She instinctively grips her arm, fingers numb. Scanning continues, detecting a brief pause in otherwise calm breathing. A red alarm: “Potential hypoxic gene. Access to high-density social areas prohibited.” She immediately leans forward, pretending to offer steady breaths, but the camera catches reddening eyes and twitching nostrils. Wearing interface glasses, Wang Li appears at the door. “Lin Yao, three failed breathing tests. Psychological assessment submitted.”

Sound: Drops, mechanical analysis, oxygen-waveform zoom, flashing alarms.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Wormhole-like rotation and simulated blood flow.
> - Composition: Overhead with red-frame highlights; blood paths visualized as red pipes.
> - Lighting: Blacklight, high-contrast biological data reflections.
> - Color: Bluish 4800K, interwoven red and blue; fear of inspection.
> - Atmosphere: Tension, surveillance, systemic violence entering the body.
> - Music: Rhythm abruptly stops; amplified buzzing and dissonant harmony.
> - Transition: In—blood-flow false-alarm pop-up; out—arm obscured by data.
> - Narrative function: Encode defects as systemic anomalies, labeling the protagonist.

### S04 Parent-child communication test · INT. · Night ｜ [02:15–03:00]

*“I'm afraid my son will cry, say the wrong thing, become ‘unqualified.’”*

Lin Yao and her five-year-old son undergo a standardized family communication test. “Mom, aren't you afraid I'll grow into a monster?” he asks. She instantly delivers the approved response: “Sweetheart, you're wonderful. You're born perfect. We must all be the best genetic children.” He asks, “Can I cry in secret?” Wang Li leans through a side door and interrupts: “Child emotional expression exceeds threshold. Begin emotional correction immediately.” His chest-mounted emotion-suppression patch activates, blue light covering his skin. Watching his eyes redden, Lin Yao forces a smile. Her voice trembles but remains standard: “Look how much we resemble the children in the template.” He cries. Infrared facial capture immediately identifies an “emotion-disrupting element.” A standard-response sound plays automatically. The child suddenly laughs, his smile rigid as a mask.

Sound: Crying cut off → replaced with standard-smile sound → “Emotional deviation successfully corrected.”

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Fixed multiple cameras, superimposed facial-emotion comparisons.
> - Composition: Three-angle symmetry, perfect-family model beside real reactions.
> - Lighting: Soft ambience; blue layer distorts the child's face.
> - Color: 5200K, desaturated blue-white; authenticity torn from control.
> - Atmosphere: Superficial harmony, inner disintegration, genuine childhood feeling erased.
> - Music: Ambience abruptly drops, replaced by synthesized standard family intonation.
> - Transition: In—correction-success prompt; out—light covers the child's face.
> - Narrative function: Extend defects to the next generation, establishing inherited oppression.

### S05 Hidden defect exposed · INT. · Night ｜ [03:00–04:00]

*“I can't take it anymore. My breathing isn't normal.”*

Alone in her dim yellow bedroom, Lin Yao tries to breathe deeply. Her chest feels nailed shut; every rapid exhalation stings. Her left fingers are numb. Tiny red vessels rise beneath her skin—classified by genetic health standards as signs of advanced pathology. Distracting herself, she opens the emotion log on her phone. Yesterday's three breathing errors reached a 43% error rate; the system has recorded an abnormal waveform trend. She bolts upright and touches her throat. Breath and heartbeat are clearly out of sync. Suddenly she recalls her mother's reddened skin at marriage registration and her father's complaints of verbal bickering. All were marked as family risk genes. She realizes she has spent her whole life pretending.

She slams the bedside control and opens a vent for air. It shuts automatically. “Illegal operation. Unauthorized ventilation causes genetic interference. Recorded as hazard level 1.” She slumps, fingers twitching, her chest expanding and contracting under metallic pressure. Outside, the community hall's perfect-gene exhibition shows Zhao Yuan's abnormal case: taken away for stuttering, still wearing an oxygen mask after discharge, branded a marginal failure.

Sound: Struggling breath, chest spasms, equipment shutting down, faint electrical current.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Breath close-up → trembling hand → skin bleeding spots → closing vent.
> - Composition: First-person macro; enlarged bodily details reveal defects.
> - Lighting: Dark yellow room, red halos at defects against cold light.
> - Color: 5000K, medium saturation, sudden red; near breakdown.
> - Atmosphere: Physical collapse, fractured self-understanding, exposed secret.
> - Music: Abrupt stop; only breath and low heartbeat remain.
> - Transition: In—push toward trembling fingers; out—vent closes.
> - Narrative function: Detonate the setup: awareness of her defects defeats suppression.

### S06 Public genetic-compliance speech · INT. · Day ｜ [04:00–05:55]

“We aren't pursuing health. We're… building a foundation on which life can never waver.”

Lin Yao must stand in the community hall as a standardized family representative. Following the training script, she says, “Since birth, I've known my happiness is measured by standards. I don't cry. I smile. I breathe according to rules. This is my freedom.” Suddenly she coughs. Ripples rise over her left lung on camera; she nearly loses control. She forces herself through the next line:

“So thank you to the Gene Program for letting every family breathe peacefully.”

Applause surges. Wang Li nods at the back. The system displays Lin Yao's emotional-standard compliance: 99.6%. A child raises a hand. “Mom, can you stop smiling?” Silence. Lin Yao blushes, then forces another smile. “Investigation initiated. Detecting emotional deviation.” The next day, a community notice announces: “Due to family emotional fluctuations, Lin Yao has been moved to the advanced psychological adaptation zone. Observation extended to 30 days.”

Returning home alone, she finds her son's bedside note: “Mom, I dreamed you said ‘don't cry,’ but you cried.” She stares, breathing rapidly, fingers trembling. Sunlight is blinding outside. At last she understands: they aren't choosing perfection. They are **drowning the reality of life in perfection**.

Sound: Applause, “Emotion regulation successful,” the child's question, paper lifted by wind.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Frontal → slow-motion cough → trembling fingers → final freeze on note.
> - Composition: Medium push-in, protagonist opposed to the perfect group.
> - Lighting: Harsh direct sunlight, reflected defect areas, strong contrast.
> - Color: 5600K, recovering saturation, dark red defects; aftershocks of complete suffocation.
> - Atmosphere: Absurd, satirical, desolate; compliance outside, collapse within.
> - Music: Main electronic rhythm returns, now dissonant; fades at the end.
> - Transition: In—floating note; out—empty shot, sunlight directly on Lin Yao's face.
> - Narrative function: Expose systemic absurdity; authenticity is consumed, perfection becomes a new prison.

**— End —**
