# Short screenplay: The Care Recipe
**Genre**: Workplace thriller / Absurdist comedy / First person / Social metaphor  
**Duration**: Approximately 7 minutes  
**Plot points**: 5  
**Directorial sensibility**: David Lynch × Elvis Presley-style absurdist realism

---

## Overall tone
- **Central idea**: Once “care” is systematized and formatted, the gentlest arrangements become the deepest exploitation and surveillance.
- **Visual style**: Cool, low-fidelity cityscapes combine warm domestic kitchens with the mechanical quality of corporate slides. Uncanny repetitions and displaced logic inhabit the details.
- **Narrative approach**: Linear progression through nested daily records, linked by first-person narration; recipes reveal key information layer by layer.
- **Point of view**: First person, Lin Mo: initially realistic and calm, gradually alienated by his environment into cognitive displacement.
- **Music**: Low electronic pulses plus cheerful cooking sounds, frying and chopping, suddenly distorted. Noise intersperses with leather-shoe footsteps; rhythm progressively deforms.
- **Emotional arc**: Calm → curiosity → fear → suspicion → assimilation: doubt disappears, and “care” is accepted as the rule.
- **Devices**:
  1. **Repetition**: The care recipe recurs with slight changes but a fixed structure, becoming ritualistic and oppressive.
  2. **Reality displaced by absurdity**: Ordinary acts such as delivering lunch acquire surreal explanations: “Do not share” and “Do not describe the taste after eating.”
  3. **Workplace emotion packages**: Corporate culture takes visible form in institutional texts: menus, attendance sheets, employee group chats.

## Characters
| Name | Identity | Appearance | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lin Mo | 32, middle-ranking marketing employee | Gray plaid shirt, perpetually polished leather shoes; slightly messy hair always combed into the standard workplace center part | Calm, orderly, trusts institutions, compulsively records things; knows the company manual by heart |
| Xiao Wang, HR | 30, administrator | Woman in a blue work jacket, leaning back in a Western office chair; always looking at a tablet, speaking extremely fast | Procedural expert; repeats phrases without emotion, mechanical intonation, briefly evasive eyes |
| The “Care Program” | Disembodied institutional personality | Appears only through recipes, notices, and system prompts | Invisible but compulsory; every action must produce an “aligned” output, every deviation becomes an anomaly record |

## Screenplay

### S01: First care recipe · INT./EXT. · Day ｜ [00:00–00:22]

*“I used to think a hot meal from the company was kindness. Later I realized it was more like a training program, teaching me to obey.”*

The kitchenette's glass door is half open. Cold-white tubes hum. Lin Mo stands by the water dispenser with an A4 printout spread beside him. Above a field of signatures, a banner reads “Employee Care Program 2024 H2.” The names form assembly lines. Parts of the paper's edge have been torn away and taped back.

The sheet contains a handwritten recipe:

> **Daily Care Menu: Lin Mo Edition**  
> 1. Sauce-fried tofu, mildly spicy—to promote nerve transmission.  
> 2. Plain rice porridge, no salt—to stabilize blood pressure and prevent anxiety responses.  
> 3. Black tea—drink after 3 seconds of bitterness to awaken concentration; do not extend.  
> 4. Check-in: paste the recipe at your desk before 13:45 and confirm completion.  
> 5. Note: if emotional fluctuation occurs during eating, immediately display an Emotional Anomaly Declaration Form for HR review.

Lin Mo's gaze stalls. His right index finger taps the paper as though confirming a reading assignment. Last week he said he wanted to resign; HR immediately sent Suggestions for Managing Emotional Fluctuation: “We recommend continuing item 16 of the care program to strengthen psychological adaptability.”

Sound: A low female broadcast loops: “Today's care program has begun. All employees must complete their assigned meals. The system will automatically record implementation.” Dripping tap, flickering fluorescent light, rustling paper.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Static medium shot, slight push to the recipe; focus on “mildly spicy” and “nerve transmission.” Oil stains and fingerprints mark the edges.
> - Composition: 45-degree overhead angle; Lin Mo lower left, orderly workstations and a whiteboard with circular key positions behind him.
> - Lighting: Cold white at 4600K, even overhead illumination with slight discontinuities; faint facial shadows weaken his eyes.
> - Color: Bright blue-white base, dark green and beige-gray recipe, restrained red lettering; cold, rusty quality.
> - Atmosphere: Distant, mechanical, faintly metallic, with a small anxiety about doing something wrong.
> - Music: Low electronic pulses beginning at 30Hz, steady and synchronized with the broadcast; a system ding every 30 seconds.
> - Transition: Slide through the kitchenette door's gap as the broadcast cuts out; a page turn carries us naturally onward without editing.
> - Narrative function: Establish the care mechanism's legitimacy and form, the protagonist's initial understanding, and institutional absurdity.

### S02: First execution · INT./EXT. · Day ｜ [00:23–01:18]

*“I checked company policy. It says ‘care’ promotes belonging. But all I'm doing is cooking.”*

Lin Mo sits by the window at his desk. Recipe open, insulated lunchbox open: square tofu, a small bowl of plain porridge, a tea bag in a glass. He follows the instructions: cuts tofu evenly with a straight blade; pours porridge exactly two-thirds full; prepares tea precisely and drinks after 3 seconds.

His watch reads 13:44. On his phone's Desk Check-in System, he taps “Today's care completed.”

> ✅ Lin Mo completed the task before 13:45. Completion this cycle: 92% (internal control threshold: ≥90%).  
> 🚨 Reminder: no emotional fluctuation was recorded during this meal. Please maintain this performance to earn the Mild Stability Award: 1 coffee voucher.

He smiles and places the lunchbox in colleague Chen Yang's drawer. Chen Yang looks up from typing and nods. “Mm. Your lunch is so bland today it could be AI-generated.”

Lin Mo nods, thinking: “Leave it. Normal people can be strange too.”

He does not mention rule five:

**“If participation involves hesitation, forgetting, or criticism, immediately submit an External Impact Record Form; standard template in Attachment 3.”**

He checks Attachment 3. One field reads: “Employees are advised to record ‘instinctive responses’ as ‘system dysfunction.’”

Sound: three quiet wok sounds; tea pouring; phone chime; announcement: “The 17th employee has completed today's care. System points +1.”

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Handheld medium shot, slowly approach the lunchbox, close-up of clearly textured tofu with blunt edges; overhead shot of the box entering the drawer, the miniature image moving inside.
> - Composition: His subjective diagonal view from upper left; food arranged ritually in alignment with the drawer grid.
> - Lighting: Yellowish daylight contrasts with cold indoor light, illuminating only the lunchbox edge; deep shadows below his waist.
> - Color: Pale-gray porridge, milky tofu, dark-amber tea; overall cool at about 4200K, food given a measurable texture.
> - Atmosphere: Positive on the surface, faintly watched; lowered head, mechanical motions, wandering eyes.
> - Music: Unchanged electronic pulse plus soft synthesized drums synchronized with check-in prompts; each action adds a tick.
> - Transition: Enter as the lunchbox is set down; pull back as he sits and meets his own gaze, hold for 1 second, cut.
> - Narrative function: Replicated behavior and internalized rules; cooking becomes a reporting task rather than life.

### S03: Anomaly discovered · INT./EXT. · Night ｜ [01:19–02:45]

*“I searched files from five years ago. The care program began as a pilot called the ‘Emotional Stability Model.’”*

After work, Lin Mo opens compartment 7 of an old filing cabinet and finds last month's Employee Mental Health Protection training records. A yellowed booklet's title page reads:

> **Emotional Stability Model (Trial Version)**  
> Objective: standardized dietary intervention to reduce anxiety, anger, and resignation tendencies arising from maladaptation to company policies.  
> Measured result: after 30 consecutive days, the test group's incidence of emotional fluctuation decreased by 67%.

On page 12, beneath a menu:

**“In test cycle 7, meal-sharing activated delirium nodes. Experiment terminated. Intergroup communication prohibited thereafter.”**

His face tightens. He remembers telling Chen Yang, “This meal is… almost too good to drink.” Chen Yang was absent the next day; his WeChat status read “Emotionally unstable; removed from post.”

Under Recipe Revision History, item 14:

**“Employees may not describe food's taste. ‘Sweet,’ ‘salty,’ ‘spicy,’ and similar terms constitute violations requiring a Sensory Suppression Training Form.”**

He slams the booklet shut, trembling. On his Personal Summary Form, his note reads: “Yesterday's bowl of reishi porridge felt like clouds when I drank it. Unreal.”

The sentence is red, annotated: “Non-recommended description. Source: Lin Mo (ID: M-321) → recorded as perceptual anomaly; cognitive correction recommended.”

He steps back into the cabinet. Pages turn outward; an A4 sheet falls. The New Recipe Trial Version is identical to today's recipe, except for its bold heading:

> **Version 18: Advanced Stability Mode**  
> 5. Do not share any ingredient or taste with others, or the Silent Feedback Mechanism will activate.

His throat tightens. In the mirror his eyes are empty, mouth corners slightly lowered, as if carrying out a standard procedure.

Sound: rustling pages; an old printer beeps inside the cabinet; distant broadcast: “Today's care completion rate is 95.3%. Employees exceeding targets receive Stability Points.”

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Subjective close-up of shaking hands, push into old files, slow-motion falling paper, then pull back to the half-open cabinet as light dims.
> - Composition: Low angle, Lin Mo right, cabinet left; restricted view shows only a document corner and hand movements.
> - Lighting: Daylight fades; cold-white room light turns dull yellow. Yellow halos on pages suggest a forgotten past.
> - Color: Yellowed documents, a warm 600K shift; the new recipe's cold green clashes with the room. “Prohibited” sits in a red box.
> - Atmosphere: Oppression and realization, close to cognitive collapse; the broadcast hums in a corner like distant current.
> - Music: Electronic pulse abruptly stops for 1.5 seconds, then a very low 50Hz hum follows the heartbeat.
> - Transition: Enter naturally as paper falls; follow his lengthening silhouette toward the kitchenette and the next scene.
> - Narrative function: Reveal care's purpose: controlling emotion and behavior, “purifying” people through rules.

### S04: A colleague's response · INT./EXT. · Day ｜ [02:46–03:42]

*“I'm not cooking anymore. I'm completing a task, like changing clothes.”*

At 10:00 the next morning, Lin Mo enters the kitchenette to check in. Chen Yang sits in a corner with a cold lunchbox identical to his: tofu, porridge, black tea.

“Lin Mo,” Chen Yang says, “you put sesame oil in today's meal. That's not quite right.”

Lin Mo recalls the explicit “no seasoning” instruction. “Yes. Standard version. No adjustments today.”

Chen Yang nods. “I asked Xiao Wang yesterday. If you don't follow the recipe, the system automatically marks you emotionally unstable. Director Li said an employee ‘claimed tomatoes are red’ and was immediately transferred.”

Lin Mo frowns. “Did that really happen?”

After a hesitation, Chen Yang whispers: “Yes. He made tomato-and-egg soup. Said, ‘The tomato is deep red, a little sweet.’ Xiao Wang made him fill in a Sensory Feedback Form. Then… he didn't come back the next day.”

Lin Mo's hand shakes. His lunchbox label says “Daily completion: 95%,” with a cursor blinking at “95%.”

Xiao Wang approaches, hands behind her back, voice level. “Lin Mo, you have completed the care program for 17 consecutive days. Stable performance. Please confirm willingness to join Standardization Training Cycle 18: preliminary food-sense training, the Real Taste Prohibition Table, and fluoride intake prompts.”

He nods. “Okay.”

She taps her tablet. A new recipe appears on the computer:

> **Cycle 18: Intensive Training Edition**  
> 1. Sauce-fried tofu, mildly spicy, using only company-supplied beans—silently recite “Confirm system permissions” before each intake.  
> 2. Plain porridge, still without salt—symbolizes purity; texture must not be described.  
> 3. Black tea—sit silently for 3 minutes afterward; no conversation.  
> 4. Check-in: completion triggers Peace-of-Mind Certification.  
> 5. **New: no sharing, mentioning, remembering, or drawing after eating. Any such act constitutes “derailment.”**

His throat tightens. He wants to say “This is wrong,” but his teeth ache as the words approach.

On his phone he sees his own Moments post:

“Had the company meal today. Strange taste, like being up in the clouds.”

A comment: “Thank you for sharing. You have triggered Sensory Stability Training. Await the next broadcast.”

He closes the phone.

Sound: Xiao Wang's fast, regular typing; a corporate promotional film on TV, employees smiling with bowls in slow motion; Chen Yang coughs; Lin Mo's breathing shallows.

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Medium shot of the two looking at each other, slow motion as Lin Mo averts his gaze; close-up of Xiao Wang's enlarged document; his trembling finger taps Confirm.
> - Composition: Symmetry, the two side by side; Lin Mo faces away, eyes lowered. Xiao Wang at left establishes controller and controlled.
> - Lighting: Chilly overall; golden sidelight emphasizes Xiao Wang, while Lin Mo's backlit face blurs, as if entering a program.
> - Color: Her device cold blue, his eating gray-white, gray-blue electronic displays behind; little sense of ordinary life.
> - Atmosphere: Intense surveillance. Collective silence is a glass curtain wall; genuine reactions become danger signals.
> - Music: Broadcast grows with the certification prompt; electronic rhythm intensifies, frequency rises toward a brain-like 10Hz.
> - Transition: Enter on Chen Yang speaking; as Lin Mo looks at his phone, darken and cut to his kitchen.
> - Narrative function: Confirm systemic suppression and the complete foreclosure of individual expression, closing the absurd loop.

### S05: Self-confirmation · INT./EXT. · Night ｜ [03:43–04:48]

*“Yesterday in the kitchen, for the first time, I didn't look at the recipe. I tasted the tofu and realized—it was actually salty. But I was afraid to say it.”*

Late at home. Lin Mo sits at the small kitchen table before the recipe printout: execution 21, dated “2025.04.05.” He intended to follow it exactly, but looks up and retrieves an old sticky note from his briefcase:

“Tofu = low sodium = mildly spicy, but actually 1.5 grams of salt added—internal control error.”

He smiles, tears it up, and throws it away.

He prepares dinner: tofu in 3-centimeter squares, each pressed with the knife's back to simulate System-Verified Cutting; porridge two-thirds full, repeated three times as Stability Reproduction practice; black tea drunk after 3 seconds, especially observing “No speaking, no thinking.”

When the third spoonful of rice is cooked, he raises it to his mouth for the first time without looking at the recipe or reciting. He simply eats.

It is salty. A thought briefly appears: **“Like Grandma's cooking back home, with the earthy taste of a wood fire.”**

He snaps his mouth shut, breathing fast, and writes:

“Execution 21. Leakage: sensory feedback triggered, unrecorded → self-suppressed; process maintained.”

Then he signs into the Employee Health Platform and selects Today's Report:

“Emotional state: stable.”  
“Dietary execution: complete.”  
“Anomalies: none.”  
“Today's feeling: warm.”

Peace-of-Mind Points rise to 99%. A system announcement appears:

“Congratulations, Lin Mo. After 21 consecutive days of care-program completion, you join the Advanced Stability Employee tier. Reward: access to the Home Cooking Simulator, for company-certified households only.”

He smiles, apparently adjusted at last, and gently pastes the recipe on the kitchen wall, its corners slightly crumpled from use. Outside, city lights stand still like an assembly line.

He picks up a pen and adds to the recipe:

**[Warming supplement suggestion]: Add one spoonful of rock sugar on day seven to improve ‘cognitive blurring’—apply for adjustment permissions in advance.**

His writing flows. Calm expression, faint smile, as if completing a work plan.

Sound: quiet stove flame; extremely slow single piano notes, slightly distorted after bar six; phone ding; repeating announcement: “System completion confirmed. You have established a stable dietary reflex.” A cheerful child's voice from outside the door: “Welcome to the Warm Family. Today's lunchtime care meal has arrived.”

> **Audiovisual notes**
> - Camera: Static long take of cooking, cut to his new recipe entry, slowly withdraw into the living room. City lights reveal a street and a small restaurant sign: “Warmth Canteen—Employees Only.”
> - Composition: Medium-long shot, Lin Mo centered, recipe held high parallel to the wall; his smiling face reflects there, contrasting with earlier emptiness.
> - Lighting: Warm kitchen yellow at 3200K against cold exterior light. His face regains color, but his eyes remain shallow, still executing tasks.
> - Color: Warm yellow and cream kitchen; dark green and blue-gray recipe. A small red mark beside the newly added “rock sugar” is piercing.
> - Atmosphere: Deep aftertaste; the uncanny satisfaction of completed domestication, like waking from hibernation.
> - Music: Piano slows and steadies, finally synchronized with the announcement. “Enter the stable sequence” is filtered with a 2-second delay, as though processed late.
> - Transition: Enter naturally through cooking; freeze on the wall recipe, red writing trembling in the wind. Slowly darken; a tiny ding, then silence.
> - Narrative function: Close the cycle from doubt through alertness and fear to self-justification. He becomes part of the system; absurdity and theme converge, leaving an aftertaste.
